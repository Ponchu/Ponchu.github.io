Pixelizi

An app to draw and learn pixel art on your phone.

  • An editor with layers, selection, classic palettes and frame-by-frame animation. Export PNG and animated GIF, or share your drawings.
  • The Learn section: 50 lessons and 70 exercises, from the first steps to characters, scenes, animation and fighting-game sprites.
  • In English and Spanish. No account needed: your drawings stay on your phone.
  • Free with ads. A single payment, "Remove ads", takes them away.
